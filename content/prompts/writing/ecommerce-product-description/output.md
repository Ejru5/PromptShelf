## Ember & Oak Soy Candle — Smoked Cedar

A slow evening in a jar. Smoked cedar and black pepper settle into warm amber and a whisper of vanilla — the kind of scent that makes a room feel lived-in.

Hand-poured in small batches in Bristol using 100% soy wax and a crackling wooden wick that sounds like a fire on a cold night.

- **Burn time:** 50–55 hours
- **Notes:** cedarwood, black pepper, amber, vanilla
- **Size:** 220 g in a reusable smoked-glass jar
- **Clean burn:** vegan, phthalate-free, cotton-free wooden wick

**Tip:** let the first burn reach the edges of the jar (about 2 hours) for an even melt every time after.
