Two years ago I shipped a feature nobody used.

Six weeks of work. A launch post. A celebratory cake.

Adoption after a month: 3%.

I was sure the problem was marketing. So I wrote better release notes. Recorded a demo. Added a tooltip.

Still 3%.

Then I did the thing I should have done first: I watched five customers try to use it.

Every single one got stuck on the same screen — the button they needed was labelled with *our* internal name for the feature.

We renamed one button.

Adoption went to 41% the next week.

What I took from it:

→ Your team's vocabulary is not your customer's vocabulary.
→ Five user sessions beat fifty opinions.
→ The fix is often embarrassingly small. That's good news.

What's the smallest change that's made the biggest difference in your work?
